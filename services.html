Good to know

Before you sit down

No — walk-ins are welcome and always have been. But evenings and weekends fill up, so booking ahead is the difference between sitting straight down and waiting for a chair.

Use the booking form on this site and it sends your request straight to the shop on WhatsApp, or just call us. You'll get a reply confirming the time.

A cut is about 30 minutes and a beard around 20. Colour, protein and straightening run from 45 minutes up to two hours — the full menu lists the time for every service.

Ask us for the current price list — prices move with product costs, so we'd rather quote you than publish something out of date.
