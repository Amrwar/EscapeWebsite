Our Story

Slow down. Sit down.

Escape Barbershop started with a simple idea: a haircut shouldn't feel like an errand. We wanted a room where the light is low, the chairs are comfortable, and nobody is looking at the clock — a place that feels less like a shop and more like a short trip away from everything else.

Every station is set up for precision. Every visit ends the same way: you leave looking better than you walked in, and feeling like you actually took a break.
