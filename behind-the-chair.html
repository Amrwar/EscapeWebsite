The standard

The same work in every chair

Four barbers, one way of doing things.

  1. 01

    We ask first

    Before anything is switched on: what shape you want, how you wear it during the week, and what your hair will actually hold. Two minutes that save a fortnight of regret.

  2. 02

    Clean tools, every client

    Blades sharpened rather than swapped out, guards cleaned between clients, fresh towels each time. Not a selling point — the baseline.

  3. 03

    The finish is the job

    A razor edge at the neck, a clean line at the temple, and a hot towel to close. The last five minutes are what people notice for the next two weeks.

  4. 04

    You leave knowing how to keep it

    Which product, how much, and how to get the same shape at home without a full kit on the shelf.
