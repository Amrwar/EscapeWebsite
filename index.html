The place

Not just a barbershop.
An escape.

Low light, dark concrete, and a chair that finally lets your shoulders drop. Escape was built for people who want their grooming to feel like a reset — sharp lines, honest service, no rush.

Every station is set up for precision: proper wash basins, hot towels, blades that are sharpened, not swapped out. Every visit ends the same way — you leave looking better than you walked in.
