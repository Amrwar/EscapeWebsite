The place

Not just a barbershop.
An escape.

Pale concrete, black steel, arched mirrors and two climbers scaling the wall above the sofa — the room was built to be worth the half hour, not just to hold a chair.

Every station is set for precision: proper wash basins, hot towels, Kérastase and L'Oréal on the shelf, blades that are sharpened rather than swapped. You leave looking better than you walked in.
